Guideline 3.1.2 - Business - Payments - Subscriptions

The submission did not include all the required information for apps offering auto-renewable subscriptions. The app metadata must include a functional link to the Terms of Use (EULA). If you use the standard Apple Terms of Use (EULA), include a link to the Terms of Use in the App Description. If you use a custom EULA, add it in App Store Connect.
